### Our Commitment

Vaulta aims to make its website and digital services accessible and usable by people with different abilities, devices, and assistive technologies.

We recognise that accessibility is an ongoing responsibility and seek to improve the experience for users with visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, and other accessibility needs.

### Accessibility Standard

Vaulta uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as its accessibility design and improvement target.

This target is not a representation of independently verified compliance. The accessibility of the platform depends on testing, implementation, third-party components, and ongoing maintenance.

### Accessibility Measures

Our accessibility efforts may include:

* Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
* Appropriate headings and semantic page structure.
* Descriptive labels for forms and controls.
* Text alternatives for meaningful images.
* Readable typography and sufficient contrast.
* Responsive layouts for different screen sizes.
* Clear validation messages and error handling.
* Compatibility testing with commonly used assistive technologies.
* Accessible account, deposit, withdrawal, and security workflows.

### Known Limitations

Some pages, charts, market-data widgets, embedded services, or third-party components may not yet provide a fully accessible experience.

Where limitations are identified, we aim to assess their impact and prioritise improvements.
