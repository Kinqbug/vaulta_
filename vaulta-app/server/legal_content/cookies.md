### 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Vaulta uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and related services.

Cookies are small files stored on a device by a website. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, and other tools that help websites operate or understand how they are used.

### 2. Why We Use Cookies

Depending on the features enabled, Vaulta may use the following categories.

#### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These support essential functions such as account authentication, session management, security, fraud prevention, and maintaining user preferences necessary for the service.

Where permitted by law, these may operate without consent when they are strictly necessary to provide a service requested by the user.

#### Preference Cookies

These remember choices such as language, display preferences, and other settings.

Where consent is required, these cookies will be used only after the applicable consent has been obtained.

#### Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with the website, identify errors, and improve performance.

Where required by law, analytics cookies will remain disabled until consent is obtained.

#### Marketing Cookies

If Vaulta uses marketing or advertising technologies, they may help measure campaigns or deliver relevant promotional content.

These technologies will be used only where lawful and with any consent required by applicable law.

### 3. Consent and Preferences

Where applicable law requires consent, Vaulta will request consent before placing or activating non-essential cookies.

You may be able to accept all optional cookies, reject them, or choose individual categories through the cookie preference interface.

Where required, you may withdraw or change your consent at any time through the cookie settings interface.

Rejecting optional cookies should not disable features that are genuinely necessary to provide the service, although some preferences or optional features may not work as expected.

### 4. Third-Party Technologies

Some technologies may be provided by external analytics, security, support, or other service providers.

The identity of these providers depends on the tools actually integrated into Vaulta.

Where required, the relevant providers and purposes will be disclosed through the cookie preference interface or an accompanying cookie inventory.

### 5. Session and Persistent Cookies

Session cookies generally expire when the browsing session ends.

Persistent cookies remain on a device for a defined period or until deleted.

Actual durations depend on the cookie's purpose and the technology used.

### 6. Browser Controls

Most browsers provide settings to view, delete, or restrict cookies.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent account login, session management, security checks, or other essential functions from operating correctly.

Browser controls may not replace a consent mechanism where applicable law requires one.

### 7. Data Protection

Information collected through cookies may be personal information where it identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

Vaulta processes such information in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

### 8. International Requirements

Cookie and tracking requirements differ between jurisdictions.

Vaulta will apply the consent, disclosure, and preference-management requirements relevant to its activities and the locations of its users.
