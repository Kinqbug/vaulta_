**IMPORTANT: CRYPTOCURRENCY AND DIGITAL ASSET TRANSACTIONS INVOLVE SIGNIFICANT RISK. YOU MAY LOSE SOME OR ALL OF THE VALUE OF YOUR INVESTMENT.**

### 1. Market Volatility

Cryptocurrency prices can rise or fall sharply within short periods. Prices may be affected by market sentiment, economic events, technological developments, regulatory announcements, liquidity, and other unpredictable factors.

You may be unable to sell an asset at your expected price or recover the amount originally invested.

### 2. No Guaranteed Profit

No investment return, price increase, yield, or profit is guaranteed.

Past performance, historical charts, projections, and examples do not reliably predict future outcomes.

Do not rely on promotional statements or third-party opinions as a guarantee of financial performance.

### 3. Loss of Capital

You may lose part or all of the assets committed to a transaction.

Only transact with funds you can afford to lose, and consider obtaining independent professional advice before making significant financial decisions.

### 4. Liquidity and Execution Risk

Some assets may have limited trading activity or insufficient buyers and sellers.

Low liquidity can cause substantial price movements, wider spreads, delayed transactions, or an inability to exit a position at a desired price.

A displayed market price does not guarantee that an order can be completed at that price.

### 5. Blockchain and Network Risk

Blockchain networks may experience congestion, outages, forks, protocol changes, technical failures, or attacks.

Transactions may remain unconfirmed for extended periods or become difficult to trace.

Transfers sent using the wrong network, address, or asset may be irrecoverable.

Blockchain transactions generally cannot be reversed simply because a sender makes a mistake.

### 6. Custody and Access Risk

Access to digital assets may depend on private keys, wallet infrastructure, custodians, authentication systems, and other technical arrangements.

Loss of access, theft, compromised credentials, insolvency of a service provider, or operational failure may result in losses.

The protection available to customer assets depends on the actual custody arrangement and applicable law.

### 7. Cybersecurity and Fraud

Cryptocurrency users may be targeted by phishing, impersonation, malware, fraudulent investment offers, social engineering, and compromised devices.

Vaulta will not ask users to disclose private keys or wallet recovery phrases as part of ordinary support.

Always verify official platform addresses and transaction details before authorising transfers.

### 8. Stablecoin Risk

Stablecoins may fail to maintain their intended value.

Their stability may depend on reserve assets, redemption mechanisms, issuers, market liquidity, and regulatory arrangements.

A stablecoin can lose its peg, become difficult to redeem, or become subject to restrictions.

### 9. Smart Contract and Protocol Risk

Smart contracts and decentralised protocols may contain vulnerabilities or design flaws.

Exploits, governance disputes, protocol changes, or failures of external dependencies may result in loss of funds.

Technical audits or security reviews do not eliminate these risks.

### 10. Regulatory and Legal Risk

Cryptocurrency laws differ between jurisdictions and may change over time.

A regulatory action, court decision, new law, sanctions restriction, or change in tax treatment may affect an asset's value, availability, transferability, or legal status.

A service that is available today may later become restricted or unavailable in a particular market.

### 11. Counterparty and Provider Risk

Transactions may depend on exchanges, custodians, wallet providers, liquidity providers, issuers, or other counterparties.

A counterparty may experience insolvency, operational disruption, security incidents, or legal restrictions.

Such events may delay or prevent access to assets.

### 12. Staking, Lending, and Yield Risk

Where offered, staking, lending, yield-generating, or similar products may involve lock-up periods, validator penalties, counterparty default, protocol failures, redemption restrictions, and uncertain returns.

Any advertised rate may be variable and may change or cease.

The risks and terms of each product must be reviewed separately before participation.

### 13. Leverage and Derivatives

Where offered and legally permitted, leveraged trading and derivatives can amplify losses.

Losses may exceed the initial amount committed, depending on the product and its terms.

Users must understand liquidation rules, margin requirements, funding costs, and other relevant conditions before using these products.

### 14. Currency and Tax Risk

Digital assets may be valued or transacted in currencies different from the user's normal spending currency.

Exchange-rate changes, conversion costs, and tax liabilities may affect the final financial outcome.

Users are responsible for understanding their applicable tax obligations.

### 15. Operational and Service Availability Risk

The Vaulta platform, external providers, and blockchain networks may experience interruptions or technical problems.

These issues may delay deposits, withdrawals, market-data updates, or transaction processing.

The platform may need to suspend a feature temporarily to protect users, investigate a security incident, or comply with legal requirements.

### 16. Independent Decision-Making

You should independently evaluate the risks, costs, and suitability of each transaction.

Do not rely solely on social media posts, community discussions, forecasts, or statements made by other users.

Where appropriate, consult a qualified financial, legal, or tax professional.

By accessing applicable cryptocurrency or investment features, you acknowledge that:

* Digital assets can be highly volatile.
* Transactions may be irreversible.
* You may lose some or all of the value committed.
* Not all digital assets or services have regulatory or investor-protection coverage.
* No return is guaranteed.
* You are responsible for understanding the risks before proceeding.

This acknowledgement does not waive any statutory right or protection that cannot lawfully be waived.
